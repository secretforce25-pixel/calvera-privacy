Privacy Policy for Calvera

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Calvera ("the app", "we") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles, what is shared with third parties, and the choices you have.

Calvera is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13.

Summary

Most of what Calvera stores stays on your device. The app additionally uses a small number of third-party services for sign-in, crash diagnostics, optional problem reports you choose to send, anonymous usage statistics, and optional rewarded advertisements. Those services are listed in full below. We do not sell your personal data.

Data stored only on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage and is never transmitted by us:

  • An optional display name you choose to enter.
  • Your gameplay progress: scores, lifetime experience points, current level, match history, achievements, and unlocked cosmetic items.
  • Your preferences: theme, operator settings, audio volumes, music selection, orientation lock, colorblind mode, and language.
  • A snapshot of an in-progress match, used only to offer resuming it.
  • A record of which one-time purchases you own.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

Calvera offers an optional Google Play Games sign-in. If you sign in, Google provides the app with a player identifier so that cosmetic entitlements, purchases, and leaderboard entries can be associated with your account. You may decline sign-in and play offline; declining disables cosmetics and online leaderboards but does not otherwise limit gameplay.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

One-time purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other payment information. We receive only a confirmation of which item was purchased.

Crash and error diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, Calvera uses:

  • Google Play Android Vitals — provided automatically by the Google Play Store for all apps. Collects crash and performance data.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — collects error, crash and problem reports. Each report includes the error message and stack trace, the app version and build, your device model and manufacturer, operating system version and build, graphics hardware, device language, time zone, available memory, and whether the device is rooted. Sentry also derives an approximate location (city and country) from your IP address, and each report carries a randomly generated installation identifier so that repeated reports from the same installation can be recognised as related.

That installation identifier is generated by the SDK, is not linked to your name, email address or Google account, and is reset if you reinstall the app. We use these reports solely to diagnose defects, and we do not use them to try to identify you personally. See the Sentry Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem yourself (optional)

Calvera includes an optional "Report a problem" form. It is never triggered automatically — it is sent only when you choose to fill it in and submit it. When you do, the report is delivered to Sentry (above) together with:

  • the description you write;
  • a screenshot of the app at the moment you opened the form;
  • technical details about the state of the game (for example the current screen, game mode and app version) and your device model and operating system version.

Your name and email address are optional fields on that form. If you leave them blank, nothing identifying you is included. Please avoid including personal information in the description. If you send a report and later want it deleted, contact us at the address at the end of this policy.

Analytics

Calvera uses GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played — for example how many players complete the tutorial, which game modes are used, and how often matches are finished. This collects a generated installation identifier, device and operating system information, country-level location (derived from IP address, not precise location), and in-app gameplay events.

Analytics events are only sent where you have given the applicable consent (see "Your choices and consent" below). We use this only to improve the game, and the reports we look at are aggregated rather than tied to an individual player. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Calvera shows rewarded advertisements only. These are always optional and always started by you: you may choose to watch a short video advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as an additional hint. Calvera does not show pop-up (interstitial) advertisements, banner advertisements, or any advertisement you did not choose to start. You can play the entire game without ever watching one.

Advertisements are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and information about your interaction with the advertisement in order to serve and measure ads.

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Your choices and consent

  • Advertising consent. Where required by law (including the EEA, the UK, and applicable US states), Calvera presents a consent screen before any personalised advertising data is collected, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You may change or withdraw your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • Advertising identifier. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Sign-in. Optional, and declinable at first launch.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data. To request deletion of data held by us, contact us at the address below.

Data retention

Crash reports and analytics data are retained for a limited period in line with the providers' standard retention settings, and are used only in aggregate or for defect diagnosis.

Children's privacy

Calvera is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

The third-party providers named above may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Those providers maintain their own safeguards for such transfers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
